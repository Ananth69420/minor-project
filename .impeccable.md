## Design Context

### Users
The student presenting the minor project and faculty reviewing it. They need to advance the fleet simulation, inspect dispatch decisions, and compare the learned policy with the baselines.

### Brand Personality
Clear, measured, practical. The interface should make the model easy to explain and its results easy to read.

### Aesthetic Direction
An academic research demo with a calm, paper-like surface, muted green accents, and compact operational data. Maps and policy details should remain the visual focus.

### Design Principles
- Keep step, play, pause, reset, and comparison controls easy to find.
- Show model state and policy decisions in plain language alongside the underlying values.
- Use restrained color to distinguish vehicle states, demand phases, and outcomes.
- Keep benchmark claims tied to the reported measurements.
