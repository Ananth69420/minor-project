Semi-Markov Decision Process (SMDP) Formulation Theoretical architecture of the fleet controller
Decision Epochs:

Asynchronous event-driven. Triggered whenever any vehicle in the fleet becomes IDLE and finishes its assigned trip or repositioning trajectory.

Shared Q-Table:

All 8 autonomous vehicles update and query a single common 576 × 6 matrix, accelerating sample efficiency by 8× through decentralized execution.

Reward Signal:

R = -0.5·N_wait + 20·N_served - 0.1·dist - 15·N_dropped

Directly penalizes passenger wait and incentivizes throughput while discouraging empty vehicle miles.

Hyperparameters:

α = 0.05 • γ = 0.96 • ε_0 = 1.0 → ε_min = 0.02 (decay 0.9996). Validated on seeds 1000–1199.

Policy Masking Rule: Actions 0 (Serve Nearest) and 1 (Serve Longest Waiting) are valid only when pending requests exist in the city. If both own-quadrant and hotspot-quadrant request buckets are zero, dispatch actions are masked out, forcing the vehicle to choose between repositioning to an anticipated high-demand zone or remaining in standby.
